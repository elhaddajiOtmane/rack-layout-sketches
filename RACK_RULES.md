# Rack planning rules I use

- One rack unit (1U) = 1.75 in (44.45 mm); standard racks are 19 in wide (EIA-310). Number U positions from the bottom up.
- Heaviest equipment goes lowest - UPS units and dense servers at the bottom keep the rack stable.
- Never plan two devices in the same U; the checker script in this repo exists because I kept doing it on paper.
- Leave room for cable management and PDUs - a full rack with no slack space is a rack you can't service.
- Blanking panels in empty U spaces keep hot air from recirculating into intakes.
- Check rail depth and door clearance before assigning a U to deep equipment.

The 12U plan in this repo is a fictional practice layout, not a real installation record.
